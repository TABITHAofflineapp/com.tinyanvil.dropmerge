Privacy Policy

Drop and Merge Numbers · Package: com.tinyanvil.dropmerge
Last updated: 4 September 2026

Privacy at a glance: Drop and Merge Numbers is designed to be usable without creating an account or submitting personal information to the developer. The current Android build requests vibration access and includes Amazon Appstore in-app purchasing components. Payment information for an Amazon Appstore purchase is handled by Amazon rather than stored by the developer.

1. Who this policy applies to

This Privacy Policy explains how TABITHA ANN REED ("we", "us", or "our") handles information in connection with the mobile game Drop and Merge Numbers (the "App").

We aim to collect only the information that is necessary to operate the App. This policy should be read together with the privacy information presented in the Amazon Appstore and any applicable laws in the jurisdictions where the App is distributed.

2. Personal information we collect

We do not intentionally collect or request personal information such as your name, postal address, telephone number, email address, contacts, precise location, photographs, microphone recordings, or government identification through the App.

The App does not require you to create an account or sign in to play. We do not operate a user profile or customer account system inside the App.

3. Game data and device storage

Game-related information, such as gameplay progress, preferences, or settings, may be stored locally on the device when required for the App's functionality. Local information is not treated by us as a copy of your personal information on our servers.

If local game data is removed by uninstalling the App, clearing the App's storage, resetting the device, or another device-level action, we may not be able to restore that information because we do not maintain a separate server-side copy of it.

4. Internet transmission, analytics, and advertising

The App is not intended to require an online account or a developer server for normal gameplay. We do not intentionally use the App to collect personal information for advertising or behavioral profiling.

The current APK review did not identify advertising or analytics SDKs such as common advertising/analytics packages. If a future version introduces advertising, analytics, crash reporting, cloud services, or another third-party service that collects user data, this Privacy Policy and the Amazon Appstore privacy disclosures will be updated before or when that functionality is introduced, as applicable.

5. Permissions

The reviewed Android package declares the Android vibration permission. Vibration may be used to provide tactile feedback during gameplay.

The reviewed manifest does not declare permissions for contacts, microphone, camera, or location access. The App therefore does not intentionally use those permissions to collect personal information.

6. Amazon Appstore and in-app purchases

The App includes Amazon Appstore in-app purchasing components. If you choose to make an in-app purchase, the transaction is processed through Amazon's purchasing infrastructure.

We do not request or store your full payment-card number, banking credentials, or other payment credentials. Payment information entered for an Amazon Appstore transaction is handled by Amazon according to Amazon's applicable terms and privacy practices.

We may receive or process purchase-related information that Amazon makes available to the App solely to provide or validate the purchased functionality, subject to the applicable Amazon Appstore APIs and policies.

7. Third-party services

The App may rely on platform components and services supplied by Amazon and the Android operating environment. Those providers may process information independently under their own privacy notices and terms.

We do not sell your personal information. We do not intentionally share personal information with advertisers or data brokers.

8. Children's privacy

The App does not require users to submit personal information in order to play. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children through the App.

If you believe that a child has provided personal information to us, please contact us using the details below so that we can review the situation and take appropriate action.

9. Data retention and deletion

Because we do not maintain a developer-operated account database for the App, we do not retain a personal profile for you on our servers.

Local game data can generally be removed through the device's App settings, by clearing the App's storage, or by uninstalling the App. Purchase records maintained by Amazon are subject to Amazon's own retention and deletion practices.

10. Security

We take reasonable measures appropriate to the nature of the App to avoid unnecessary collection and disclosure of information. Because local game data is stored on your device, the security of that local information also depends on the security controls of your device and operating system.

11. Your privacy choices

You can choose not to provide personal information to the App. You can also remove locally stored App data using the device controls described above.

If you have a privacy request or believe information has been collected in a manner inconsistent with this policy, you may contact us at aradiasidonieec@hotmail.com.

12. Changes to this Privacy Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy when the App's functionality, services, legal requirements, or data practices change. The "Last updated" date at the top of this page will be changed when material updates are made.
